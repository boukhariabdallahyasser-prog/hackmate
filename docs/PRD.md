# Product requirements

HackMate serves students, event organizers, and moderators across Algerian universities. The English launch offers an open event directory and a verified student workspace for team formation.

- Anyone can open a shared published event. Listings disclose whether the community, organizer, or an administrator supplied verification.
- Students register without invitations using an approved university email domain. Confirming email and passing a manual evidence review are separate steps.
- Verified students can publish event submissions for moderation, find peers, create teams, apply, and invite. Team leadership belongs to a team rather than to a global account role.
- Verification evidence stays private. Profile access is limited to verified members, the owner, and authorized staff. Optional information is controlled by its owner.
- Moderators handle assigned content. Admins operate verification, the university registry, and community workflows. Super admins manage privileged roles and critical settings.
- Measure registrations, verification turnaround, event publication, and active teams from actual records. Report an empty platform honestly during onboarding.
